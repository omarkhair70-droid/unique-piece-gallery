# The Unique Piece Art Gallery — القطعة الفريدة

**An Arabic-first digital art-gallery and private-commission experience for presenting handcrafted work and converting custom requests through WhatsApp.**

[Live website](https://unique-piece-gallery.vercel.app)

> **Positioning:** supporting creative/commercial web work. It is not part of the primary flagship lineup.

## Experience

The site is structured as a digital exhibition rather than a conventional product grid. It combines:

- Arabic/English art-gallery identity;
- craft-domain storytelling;
- curated artwork presentation;
- commissioned-work inquiry;
- size, material, description, and budget capture;
- WhatsApp handoff for custom orders;
- direct phone and map/contact actions.

## Craft domains represented

The content covers paintings, watercolor, handmade work, relief murals, institutional art, Arabic calligraphy teaching, waterfalls/fountains, and modern/classic wall art.

## Commission flow

```text
Gallery / story
      ↓
Private commission form
      ↓
Work type + size + material + brief
      ↓
Optional budget
      ↓
Pre-filled WhatsApp request
```
